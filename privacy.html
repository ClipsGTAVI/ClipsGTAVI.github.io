Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This tool is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's TikTok account solely to upload videos to the owner's drafts, using TikTok's official API.

The account's access token is stored locally on the owner's computer and nowhere else. The tool does not collect, sell or share personal data from any other person.

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings under Security → Manage app permissions.
